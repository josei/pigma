Open source · runs in your browser

Design in the browser, nothing to install.

Pigma is a Figma-inspired design editor: vector tools, real layers, auto layout, components, prototypes and export, in a tab you already have open.

Open the editor → View the source

Your work is saved in this browser. Export a file to keep a copy or move it.

Everything a pig needs to design

Six tools you reach for every day, all in one editor.

V

Vectors and shapes

Draw rectangles, ellipses and paths, then combine them with boolean operations that stay live.

L

Layout that adapts

Auto layout, grids and constraints reflow your frames when content or size changes.

C

Components

Make a component once, place instances, and override only what should differ.

P

Prototypes

Link frames, add overlays and smart animate, then present full screen.

E

Export

Export SVG, PNG and PDF from any selection, at the scale you need.

AI assistant (MCP)

Ask Claude to draw it

Claude connects to Pigma through the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and edits your design with Pigma's tools: frames, text, fills. You describe it, and the canvas changes.

The assistant needs a Pigma server. The desktop app runs one on your machine.

Figma files

Bring your Figma files along

Open a Figma .fig file or a Figma REST export in Pigma, keep editing, and export a .fig back out. Pigma lists anything it cannot map in an import report.
